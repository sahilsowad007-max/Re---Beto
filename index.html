Why not public AI

Your staff already use AI. The question is whose.

Every week someone pastes a customer file, a board paper or a loan memo into a public chatbot. A private AI gives them the same help without the leak.

  • It leaves the countryPublic AI runs on servers abroad, under foreign law.
  • It sits under someone else's termsThe vendor decides what it keeps, and for how long.
  • You cannot audit itNo record of who asked what, or what went out.
  • Your regulator will askBanks and ministries must show where their data lives.

What private AI means

Three promises, built in from the first layer.

01

Private

Your data never leaves your boundary and trains nobody else's model.

  • No training on your data
  • Your documents, your answers
  • Every answer logged
02

Sovereign

In your country, under your law, with your keys. No lock-in to a model vendor.

  • Runs in Bangladesh or in your data centre
  • You hold the keys
  • Switch models without a rebuild
03

Made for you

Your knowledge, your systems, your language and your rules.

  • Bangla and English, typed or spoken
  • Connected to your ERP, CRM, core banking
  • Your name, your brand, your guardrails

Questions

What CIOs ask us first.

Is our data used to train anyone else's AI?

No. Your documents answer your people and your customers. That is all they do.

Where does it run?

On dedicated GPUs on QSor in Dhaka, in your own cloud, or on your own servers. We agree this in the first meeting, before anything is loaded.

Which models does it use?

Open models such as Llama, Qwen and DeepSeek, tuned on your knowledge, or a commercial model through your own gateway and policy. You can switch the model later without rebuilding the AI.

Can we use our own GPUs?

Yes. Or start on dedicated GPUs on QSor and move to your own later. Your knowledge and settings move with you.

Who can see what?

Your single sign-on and your roles decide. A teller sees what a teller needs, and every question and answer is logged.

Does it speak Bangla?

Bangla and English, typed or spoken, including Bangla written in English letters.

What does it cost?

It depends on where it runs, which models it uses and how many people use it. We scope it with you and put the price in writing.
